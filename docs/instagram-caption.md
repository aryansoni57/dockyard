Dockyard is a smart dock for KDE Plasma 6.

⏱ clock, world clocks, Pomodoro
📊 CPU and memory, network speed, battery
🎵 now playing
🌤 weather
✅ todos and notes
📶 Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Night Light toggles

Plus a launcher that does maths, Meta+1 to Meta+9 for your apps, and Meta+V clipboard history.

Every tile is optional. Keep what you use.
