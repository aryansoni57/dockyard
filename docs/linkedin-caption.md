I built a dock for my Linux desktop, and I wanted it to do more than hold app icons.

Dockyard is a smart dock for KDE Plasma 6. It runs inside plasmashell as a plasmoid, so there is no extra process to keep alive.

In the video the dock starts empty, and each tile drops in one at a time:
→ Start, system stats, now playing and your apps
→ Weather, Pomodoro, world clocks, todos and notes
→ Network speed, quick toggles, calendar, month progress, battery and clock

The second half is what you don't see on the bar itself:
→ A launcher that also does maths in the search box
→ Wi-Fi networks you can connect to, disconnect from and forget
→ Todos you can complete, restore and delete
→ Several notes in one tile
→ Meta+1 to Meta+9 to open or switch to your dock apps
→ Meta+V clipboard history
→ Four clock styles and five settings pages

Every tile is optional, so you keep only the ones you use.

It's open source and still changing. If you run Plasma, I'd like to know which tile you'd add next.
