# Dockyard reel storyboard

Portrait 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, about 36 s. For Instagram Reels and LinkedIn. No audio track; add music in the app.

All visuals are drawn fresh as vector scenes from the QML modules (sizes, colors and labels from `docs/STYLE.md` and `package/contents/ui/modules/`). No screenshots from earlier videos.

## Act 1: the empty dock (0.0–2.4 s)

| Time | Shot | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.2 | Dark wallpaper with soft color fields. An empty glass bar sits across the frame in perspective, receding to the right. | Title fades in above: **Dockyard** |
| 1.2–2.4 | Camera dollies in to the left end of the bar. | Subtitle: *A smart dock for KDE Plasma 6*. The title lifts away. |

## Act 2: tiles fall in (2.4–18.4 s)

The camera tracks left to right along the bar without stopping. As it reaches each empty slot, that tile drops from above, lands with a small bounce and a light ping on the bar, and stays. A caption below the bar names it. About 1 s per tile.

The full dock returns small at the bottom. **Dockyard** and *A smart dock for KDE Plasma 6* hold in the center.

## Safe areas

Instagram covers the bottom ~320 px with the caption and the right ~120 px with buttons. Captions sit between y 1180 and 1500, and nothing important sits in the right 120 px.
